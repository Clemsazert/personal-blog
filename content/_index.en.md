## Welcome to my blog

My name is Clément, and I have been a software engineer for six years.

Here, I share technical articles on software engineering, infrastructure, and the tools I use on a daily basis.

Whether you're looking for in-depth analyses of specific technologies or practical advice, I hope you'll find what you're looking for here.

This blog and the articles it contains are posted without pretension, but with the heartfelt desire to share what I discover
on a daily basis, whether in my work or my personal projects.

Any feedback is welcome to improve the quality of the blog and posts. So please feel free to contact me with your feedback, suggestions for improvement,
or even just to chat if you are interested in the topics covered (whether you agree with what I say or not!).

Happy reading!
